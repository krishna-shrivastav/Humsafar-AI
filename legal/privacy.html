Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026 · Document status: Production template requiring organisation review.

NagrikSetu is a heritage discovery and visitor assistance website. This template must be reviewed and completed with the operating organisation's legal name, contact address, grievance contact and applicable jurisdiction before public launch.

Information we may process

  • Account identifiers and email supplied by Firebase Authentication.
  • Profile preferences and language settings.
  • Images or other files that a visitor intentionally submits for a requested feature.
  • Questions and prompts submitted to heritage assistance features.
  • Feedback and corrections submitted by visitors.
  • Optional analytics events only after the visitor has opted in and authenticated.
  • Basic technical logs needed to protect and operate the service.

Purpose and sharing

Information is used to provide account access, requested heritage features, visitor support, content moderation, security, and (only with consent) aggregated service analytics. Submitted content may be sent to configured infrastructure or model providers to fulfil the requested feature. The operator must list the actual providers, locations and retention periods before launch.

Location and camera

Browser location and camera/microphone access are requested by the browser only when a visitor chooses a feature that needs them. Location should not be stored as a visitor trail by default. Do not enable background tracking without a separate, clear opt-in and legal review.

Retention and security

Use HTTPS, least-privilege access, encrypted storage, short-lived upload URLs, access logs, backups and tested deletion procedures. Define retention periods for accounts, uploaded files, questions, feedback, security logs and backups before production. Never put service-account keys or provider secrets in website code.

Visitor choices

Visitors may decline optional analytics. Account access and data deletion requests must be handled through the operator's published support channel. The operator must implement and test deletion across the account database, object storage, analytics systems and backup lifecycle.

Children

If the service is intended for children or collects information from children, obtain a jurisdiction-specific legal and safety review and implement age-appropriate notices and controls before enabling accounts or uploads.

Changes

Publish a version and effective date for each material change. This starter template is not legal advice and is not a substitute for a completed legal review.
